# ModernWork Elevate 1.4.0

See the roles that are waiting for an approver, and know the moment they are approved.

## New

- **Waiting for approval.** Every request of yours that an approver has not decided yet is listed
  above ACTIVE — directory roles, PIM groups and Azure roles — with the tenant, the scope and when
  you asked. The list comes from Microsoft, so it is still there after a restart and also shows
  requests you made in the Entra or Azure portal.
- **Notified when it is decided.** While something is waiting, the app checks every 15 seconds.
  Approved, denied, expired or canceled — you get a notification, and an approved role moves to
  ACTIVE with its countdown straight away.

## Improved

- **Safer links.** The app opens a link in your browser only when it is a complete `https` address.
