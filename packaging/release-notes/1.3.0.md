# ModernWork Elevate 1.3.0

Activate Microsoft Entra roles, PIM groups and Azure resource roles from the notification area —
built for managed Windows 11 devices.

## New

- **One list for every tenant.** Directory roles, PIM groups and Azure resource roles of all your
  admin accounts, one card per tenant. Roles reached through several paths appear once.
- **Windows sign-in only.** Windows Hello, passkeys and your organization's sign-in page through
  the Windows account broker. No password field, and no tokens are ever written to disk.
- **Managed by your organization.** Installed per device with an MSI, updated through your
  software distribution, tenants pre-configured by policy.
- **Windows 11 design.** Native corners and shadow, the Windows font and icons, and your accent
  colour in light and dark mode.
- **Your settings stay yours.** They live in your profile and cannot be read by other users of
  the device.
